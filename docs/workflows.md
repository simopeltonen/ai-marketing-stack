# AI Marketing Stack — Workflows

## Overview

The AI Marketing Stack connects AI agents with automation workflows, structured data and collaboration tools.

The purpose of the workflow layer is to move information between systems and reduce repetitive manual work.

A typical workflow follows a pattern such as:

**Trigger → Collect → Analyse → Structure → Store → Distribute → Learn**

Different workflows use different parts of this pattern depending on their purpose.

## Competitive Intelligence Workflow

One of the first practical workflows developed for the stack focuses on competitive intelligence.

The objective is to create a recurring flow of relevant market and competitor information without requiring manual research every morning.
